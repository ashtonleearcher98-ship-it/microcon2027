01 / North America

San Diego, California

Conference venue

The Ultimate Skybox

The Saturday conference takes place on the 15th floor of DiamondView Tower, 350 Tenth Avenue, San Diego, CA. The penthouse overlooks Petco Park and the skyline, with an open-air patio.

Venue details ↗
Getting there

Downtown San Diego

Plan your route to 350 Tenth Avenue. The host lists the venue near Petco Park. Confirm transit, parking and accessibility needs directly before travel.

Travel details ↗
Other locations

Reception, yacht and excursions

The yacht gala is on San Diego Bay. The Mexico trip is a cross-border coach excursion. The welcome reception location and Sunday excursion details remain subject to host announcement.

02 / Europe

Aigues-Mortes, France

Conference venue

Salle L’Oustaou

The conference venue is at 2 Rue Alsace Lorraine, 30220 Aigues-Mortes. Lunch for conference ticket holders is listed at Le Minos near Place Saint-Louis.

Venue details ↗
By rail or road

Connections via Nîmes and Montpellier

The host suggests the regional train from Nîmes to Aigues-Mortes, bus 106 from Montpellier, bus C32 from Nîmes, or the A9 motorway via Gallargues and D979. Verify current timetables before booking.

Getting there ↗
Arrival and parking

Airports and RVs

Montpellier and Nîmes are the nearby airports named by the host. Its location guide also notes approximately 50 RV spaces in Car Park 4 near the conference venue.
