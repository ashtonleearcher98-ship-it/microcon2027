MICROCON 2027 / NORTH AMERICA

San Diego,
California.

August 5–8, 2027 · Hosted by the Republic of Slowjamastan, led by Sultan Randy Williams.

Limited conference seats listed by the host. The Ultimate Skybox has a 120-seat cap. Conference with lunch is $240 USD, and the yacht gala is a separate $240 USD ticket.

01 / Event details

The San Diego experience

Venue

The Ultimate Skybox

A penthouse event space atop DiamondView Tower, 350 Tenth Avenue, overlooking Petco Park and the San Diego skyline.

Across the border

Mexico excursion

Friday’s escorted day trip visits Puerto Nuevo. The host says a passport is required; the excursion has its own waitlist.

On the water

Yacht gala

The Saturday evening dinner and gala takes place on a chartered yacht in San Diego Bay. Separate ticketing applies.
