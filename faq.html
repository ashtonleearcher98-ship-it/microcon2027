01 / Booking

Tickets and availability

North America

Is the San Diego event sold out?

The host announced the main conference was sold out in June 2026, then listed a small number of conference and yacht gala seats on its later registration page. Availability changes; check the live checkout. The Mexico excursion remains waitlisted.

Check US availability ↗
Waitlist

How do I request a released seat?

Email info@slowjamastan.org with your name, phone number and desired events. The host also recommends its WhatsApp group and newsletter for announcements. An email is a request, not a guaranteed reservation.

Official waitlist instructions ↗
Europe

Which bundle should I choose?

EU tickets include individual conference, guinguette and gala passes; two-event bundles; and a full bundle that also includes the Sunday guided tour. See each card on the ticket page for direct Stripe checkout.

Official EU registration ↗
Checkout

Where is payment processed?

The ticket buttons open the organiser-provided Stripe checkout pages. Prices and available quantities are subject to the live booking engine. Read the relevant host policy before paying.

02 / Attending

Travel and participation

Venues

Where is the conference?

US: The Ultimate Skybox, DiamondView Tower, 350 Tenth Avenue, San Diego. EU: Salle L’Oustaou, 2 Rue Alsace Lorraine, 30220 Aigues-Mortes. Other event meeting points can differ.

Travel and venues ↗
Children

Can minors attend?

The North America host permits all ages but requires under-18 guests to remain with a ticketed parent or guardian. Confirm any Europe-specific family questions directly with the EU organiser.

Delegations

How do I find attending nations?

The searchable registry lists the nations and delegate counts supplied by the hosts. Counts are a publication snapshot and can change as registrations update.

Remote participation

Can my flag appear if I cannot travel?

The North America host offers limited spaces in the printed programme for micronations unable to attend. Review its flag submission page for details and current pricing.
