04 / North America archive

Announcements and participation

June 2026

Waitlist and sold-out notice

The host announced that its conference had filled and opened a waitlist for released places. The Mexico coach also filled; a second bus may be considered if enough people enquire. Send your name, phone number and preferred events to info@slowjamastan.org. The later booking page listed a handful of conference and yacht seats, so always check the live checkout. Ticket options ↗
