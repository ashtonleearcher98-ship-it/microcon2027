MICROCON 2027 / POLICIES

Know before
you book.

Information for the San Diego and Aigues-Mortes gatherings.

01 / North America

Ticket and refund conditions

Final sale and transfers

Tickets are assigned to the purchaser

The organiser states that ticket purchases are final and cannot be refunded for personal circumstances such as schedule conflicts, illness, travel disruption or visa issues. Tickets cannot be resold, gifted or reassigned. If the entire event is cancelled, the organiser promises a full ticket refund; if rescheduled, buyers may attend the new date or choose a full refund.

Minors and conduct

Admission carries conditions

Every under-18 attendee must remain with a ticketed parent or guardian. Guests removed for breaching event rules receive no refund. The host also publishes liability, risk and force majeure conditions. The past student discount is closed.

02 / Europe

Ticket and refund conditions

Final sale and transfers

Check your selection before paying

The European organiser likewise states that tickets are final and non-transferable, including when travel or personal plans change. It promises a full refund if the event is cancelled altogether. After a reschedule, buyers may use their ticket on the new date or seek a full refund.

Participation terms

Conduct and risk

The host’s policy describes participation risks, liability limits, disruption beyond the organiser’s control and removal without refund for misconduct. For questions on the interpretation of any condition, contact the relevant organiser before purchase.

03 / Both summits

Conduct at MicroCon

Respect and credentials

Treat people and venues with care

The published codes apply to delegates, speakers, vendors and volunteers. They prohibit harassment, fighting, illegal drugs, indoor smoking or vaping, and unapproved real or replica weapons. Respect boundaries and property, wear the official badge visibly at the conference, and report concerns to event staff.

Enforcement

Warnings and removal

The hosts describe escalating responses from a staff warning to removal for the day and then for the weekend. Serious harassment or physical altercations can lead to immediate expulsion. Organisers may refuse entry or remove a person where safety or code compliance is at issue.

Booking notice: This page records the key conditions published by the hosts as of September 2026. The contract presented at checkout governs a purchase. Contact the appropriate organiser before payment if you require the complete terms or have a dispute.
