Four days.
Two worlds.

A closer look at the conferences, gatherings, excursions and galas across both continents. Times shown are local and may change.

01 / North America

San Diego experiences

Thursday · August 5

Nemean Games and welcome reception

The Nemean Games run 11:00–14:00 with a coastal setting. An opening reception is listed for 17:00–19:00; its venue is still to be announced.

Full US schedule ↗
Friday · August 6

Mexico coach excursion

A luxury coach trip is scheduled 10:00–16:00, with stops including Friendship Park and Puerto Nuevo. Lunch is planned, with an optional lobster upgrade listed by the host. A valid passport is required. This excursion is fully booked; ask the organiser about the waitlist and possible second bus.

Excursion details ↗
Saturday · August 7

Conference with lunch

A full day of presentations at The Ultimate Skybox runs 09:00–16:30. The host describes talks, networking, lunch and a closing awards ceremony. Uniforms are welcome; the host lists all ages with rules for minors.

Conference details ↗
Saturday evening

Yacht dinner and gala

Boarding begins at 18:30 for an evening charter on San Diego Bay; the host describes a cruise around 19:00–21:30 and an overall gala slot through 22:00. Dinner and refreshments are included in the separate gala ticket.

02 / Europe

Aigues-Mortes experiences

Friday · September 24

Guinguette on the Constance

A relaxed opening gathering aboard the Constance boat from 18:00–20:00, with French food, local wine and music. This is a separately ticketed experience or included in selected bundles.

EU programme ↗
Saturday · September 25

Conference and lunch

The conference at Salle L’Oustaou is scheduled 09:00–17:30. Registration includes presentations, exhibition space, drinks and snacks during breaks, and lunch at Le Minos.

Conference details ↗
Saturday evening

Gala and Golden Flamingos

The formal Golden Bucket Gala and Golden Flamingo Awards are scheduled from 19:30 to midnight. The host lists a seasonal dinner and asks guests to raise dietary needs with the organiser. The precise gala venue awaits announcement.

Gala details ↗
Sunday · September 26

Guided town tour

An organiser-led walk through Aigues-Mortes is listed, with time to be announced. The full diplomatic bundle includes the tour; a separate tour ticket is marked coming soon.
